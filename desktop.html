Propane Desktop

The desktop that builds its own tools.

Propane Desktop is an AI GUI runtime for your home computer. Open a workspace, describe your work, and it grows the tool decks you need — then hands them to your phone.

Get early accessExplore Minion
Workspaces

One process. A window for every job.

Point Propane at a folder and it opens a workspace rooted there. Every workspace registers itself in your home window, so all your work shows up in one place.

A window per project

Run the app once. New launches focus the right window or open a new one — no duplicate processes, no lost state.

Projects as single files

Each project is a .projent file — a small YAML document that carries its name, stack and context. Move the file and the project moves with it.

A real desktop app

Native on macOS, Windows and Linux, with a system tray, quick actions and global shortcuts. Built on Bun, React and Electrobun.

Tool decks

It generates the tool you need.

Tell Propane what you do and it builds the buttons, pages and views to match. Every tool deck is a small app you can run, rearrange or open up and edit.

Describe it in plain words

Ask for a client-delivery deck, a morning scan or a performance view. Propane assembles it from the pieces already in your workspace.

Actions and processes

Run scripts, chain dependencies, capture output and watch long-running processes — all from a button you asked for.

Open the code

Tool decks are small apps. Open the code editor and change anything, or build one from scratch.

Remote access

Your desktop, on your phone.

Propane runs on the machine where your files live. Mobile clients reach back to that machine, so the tools you generated at your desk are the same tools you reach for on the sofa — no separate app to maintain, no second copy of your data.

The connection is yours: pair a device, and your tools travel with you. It is part of the early-access build.

Pair a device
Scan once. Your workspaces appear on the phone.
Run from anywhere
Trigger the same actions you have at your desk.
Stays local
Your files never leave the machine they live on.
Local-first

Your machine, your models, your data.

Propane is built to run at home. The engines run locally through Minion, state lives in a small SQLite file, and projects are plain files on disk.

Runs on your hardware

Bundled local models mean the assistant works without sending your work to a cloud service.

Files, not a black box

Projects, settings and app data are readable files under your home folder. Back them up, sync them or read them yourself.

Bring your own model

Use the bundled model, or point Propane at your own. The runtime stays the same.
