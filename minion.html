What's inside

Everything a computer needs to understand you — and to create.

Minion packages the models and the plumbing around them into one open runtime, so the app on top only has to say what it wants.

Speech

Speech to text, text to speech and voice cloning — running on the machine, not a server.

Vision

Read what is on screen — windows, text and controls — so the assistant can act on it.

Model

A bundled local language model for reasoning, drafting and turning intent into a plan.

Image

Generate pictures from a prompt — on the machine, with no cloud round trip.

Video

Generate and edit short video locally, then drop it straight into your workspace.

Open

The stack is open source. Read it, fork it, ship it inside your own desktop app.

Layers

From the microphone to the picture.

Each layer does one thing and hands off to the next. You can use the whole stack, or pull out the one piece you need.

Because the layers are separate, a voice assistant, a screen reader and an image generator all run on the same foundation.

Local-first

Runs on your machine, stays on your machine.

Minion is built for home computers and desktops, not a data centre. Models download once and run locally, so your voice and your screen never have to leave the device.

No round trip

Speech, vision, language, image and video all run on-device, so results come back at the speed of your hardware.

Private by default

Your microphone and screen stay local unless you deliberately connect something.

Swap the model

Use the bundled model or bring your own. The stack underneath does not change.

For builders

Use it in your own app.

Minion is a runtime, not just Propane's engine. Use it anywhere you need a model to listen, see, understand or create — a desktop app, a server, or your own product.
